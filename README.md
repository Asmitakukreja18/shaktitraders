# Shakti Traders - Premium Wholesale Hub & Distributor Network

Welcome to **Shakti Traders Wholesale Hub**, a digital portal for B2B wholesale distribution of luxury bathware, sanitaryware, brass fittings, tiles, and home interior essentials.

## Features
- **Interactive Wholesale Catalog**: Browse product collections, specifications, MOQs, and bulk tiered pricing.
- **Brand Showcases**: Authorized distributor catalog featuring leading bathware & hardware brands.
- **Dealer Partnership Portal**: Application workflow for new franchise & dealership onboarding.
- **Real-Time Search & Filters**: Fast product filtering by material, brand, finish, and application.

## Tech Stack
- **Frontend**: HTML5, Vanilla JavaScript, Modern Tailwind-inspired CSS with custom glassmorphism components.
- **Assets**: High-resolution product images and brand documentation.

## Project Structure
- `index.html` - Main landing showcase & company profile.
- `wholesale-catalog.html` - Full B2B wholesale catalog with quick inquiry modal.
- `collections.html` - Product categories (Faucets, Sanitaryware, Tiles, Accessories).
- `brands.html` - Partner brands & manufacturing credentials.
- `become-dealer.html` - Onboarding form for prospective dealers & retail partners.
- `about.html` - Company heritage, distribution logistics & warehouse network.
