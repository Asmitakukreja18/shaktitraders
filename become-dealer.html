B2B Terms & FAQ

What is the Minimum Order Quantity (MOQ)?

expand_more

For wholesale accounts, we maintain a flexible MOQ starting at 50 meters per fabric type. Custom dye-lots require a minimum of 200 meters.

Do you offer credit terms for established retailers?

expand_more

Yes, after three successful transactions within six months, registered dealers can apply for Net-30 payment terms subject to credit approval.

Can I request customized textile patterns?

expand_more

Exclusivity is one of our key perks. We offer custom weaving and printing for orders exceeding 500 meters per design with a dedicated design consultant.
