1998

The Genesis

Shakti Traders was founded in the heart of the textile district with a simple mission: to provide artisanal quality fabrics to independent retailers. Starting with just two looms and a vision for excellence.

2012

Digital Expansion

Scaling beyond regional boundaries, we integrated computerized quality control and expanded our footprint into international markets, becoming a trusted partner for global fashion houses.

Present

Shakti Royale

Today, we stand as a leader in premium textile B2B solutions. Shakti Royale represents the pinnacle of our journey—combining royal textile heritage with modern digital efficiency for the discerning dealer.
